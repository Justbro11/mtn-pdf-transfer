Task 2: Write an article about teenage crime. (6 pts)

Why Do Some Teenagers Commit Crimes?

The number of crimes committed by teenagers is growing in some societies. There are several causes for this problem.

First, poverty and unemployment push some young people to steal. Second, many teenagers drop out of school and have nothing to do, so they spend their time in the streets with bad friends. Moreover, violent films and video games may influence their behaviour. Finally, some parents do not pay enough attention to their children.

In conclusion, families, schools and society must work together to protect our teenagers.

Exam Tips and Key Vocabulary

What this exam tests

The reading text of the July 2018 Arts exam is about WhatsApp, its founders Jan Koum and Brian Acton, and its success story. The paper tests Reading, Language and Writing, so read the text twice before answering and base every answer on the text.

Key vocabulary from the text

  • messaging application: تطبيق مراسلة
  • privacy: خصوصية
  • intimacy: حميمية
  • founder: مؤسس
  • discouraged: محبط
  • persuade: يقنع
  • tremendous: هائل
  • launch: يطلق
  • acquire: يستحوذ على
  • acquisition: استحواذ

Grammar points in this exam

  • Word formation (illiteracy, universal, financial, sustainable)
  • Gerund after how about, collocation make effort, whose
  • Present, past simple and past perfect
  • Conditional type 3, passive and reported speech
  • Language functions (complaining, clarification, disagreement)

Revise these structures: the Language section of this exam is built on them.

Writing tips

The writing paper has two tasks. In task 1, answer your friend's questions about your future plans using will or going to. In task 2, write an article explaining the causes of teenage crime.
