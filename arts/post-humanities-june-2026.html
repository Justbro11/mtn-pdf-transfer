Moroccan football has prospered in recent years at all levels. The national team's heroic performance in FIFA World Cup-2022 started a series of astonishing achievements.

[1] The accomplishments of Moroccan football are the result of a long-term project which started with the National Sport Conference in 2008, where King Mohammed VI launched a huge project to modernize Moroccan sports. The country established Mohammed VI Football Academy at a cost of $13 million, aiming mainly at developing football at the national level by identifying and training talents. The academy provides education as well as sporting development, with study spaces, player lodgings and physical preparation zones.

[2] Investing in football was key to transforming the sports sector in Morocco. The national federation prioritized developing the sporting infrastructure in the country, building new stadiums and renovating the existing ones. More than 8.7 billion dollars is invested in railways up to now. The country has invested 41 billion dollars in projects to host the African Cup of Nation-2025 and the World Cup-2030. In 2025, the Morocco-2030 Foundation was established to manage the organization of the 2025 AFCON and the 2030 World Cup in terms of planning, infrastructure and financial governance.

[3] The organization of major competitions represents a diplomatic gain for Morocco. Faithful to its African identity, Morocco makes its stadiums and infrastructure available to CAF and several African nations, believing that Africa progresses when its nations advance together. The opening of the new Prince Moulay Abdellah Stadium in a match between Morocco and Niger symbolizes the country's promise to support the growth of Africa.

[4] Hosting international events provides the country with large tourism revenues and economic investments. Many African federations regard the country as a source of inspiration. Moroccan fans earned global respect for their country through their high level of civilization. They are looked upon with great respect, as descendants of an honored civilization.

[5] Morocco's message to the world is clear: its ambition is not only to win matches, but to win hearts, minds, and friends. By opening its doors to African citizens in different competitions, the Kingdom shows its identity through excellence and solidarity.

Task 2: Write an article about helping people in need. (6 pts)

Helping People in Need

Last month, my friends and I organized a charity campaign at our school. We collected food, clothes and toys from our neighbors and classmates, and then we gave them to a local association that helps poor families. We were very happy to see the smiles on the children’s faces.

Helping others is important for several reasons. First, it makes people in need feel that they are not alone. Second, it creates solidarity and strengthens relationships in society. Moreover, it teaches us to be generous and responsible.

In conclusion, even a small gesture can change someone’s life, so we should all take part in such actions.

Exam Tips and Key Vocabulary

What this exam tests

The reading text of the June 2026 Humanities exam is about the rise of Moroccan football and the investments behind it. The paper tests Reading, Language and Writing, so read the text twice before answering and base every answer on the text.

Key vocabulary from the text

  • prosper: يزدهر
  • achievement: إنجاز
  • talent: موهبة
  • lodgings: سكن / إقامة
  • infrastructure: بنية تحتية
  • revenues: مداخيل
  • inspiration: إلهام
  • solidarity: تضامن
  • emissions: انبعاثات
  • discrimination: تمييز

Grammar points in this exam

  • Phrasal verbs and relative pronouns (take part, go over, which)
  • Gerund after “feel like”
  • Word formation (harmful, guidance)
  • Past perfect and future perfect
  • Passive voice, reported suggestions and wish + past perfect
  • Language functions

Revise these structures: the Language section of this exam is built on them.

Writing tips

The writing paper has two tasks. In task 1, continue the paragraph about how your best friend has changed. In task 2, write an article about how you and your friends helped people in need and explain why helping others is important.
