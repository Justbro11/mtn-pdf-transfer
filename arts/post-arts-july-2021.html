[2] Minnesota Virtual Academy in USA is entirely online since 1999, The academy is a public school that serves about 2,800 students. Shelley Gates, assistant principal, said that the academy is unique because many students can avoid bullying, misbehavior, lack of concentration and any other barriers that can prevent learning. “We have noticed that this online experience is successful. When students eliminate outside distractions from their learning environment, they focus more and do well,” she added.

[3] Ted is now a student at the academy. “In my previous school, I found it hard to stay engaged and I didn’t feel supported by my teachers. My family and I knew we had to do something about it. My sister had already joined Minnesota Virtual Academy, and she was doing well academically and mentally throughout the pandemic. So, I decided to move to the same school. It’s been great so far. I feel secure, healthy and I’m learning better.” he said.

[4] Indiana Digital Learning School (IDLS) is another online public school, serving primary and secondary school students. This year, an additional 2,000 students enrolled because of COVID-19. “Our students have a full-time schedule from Monday to Friday and they attend a minimum of five classes a day,” said Lewis, the school principal. The school also provides students with all the supplies they need to ensure they learn at no cost. All students get a laptop, a printer, books and a science kit. Diana, a university teacher, has just moved her daughter Ann to the school. Ann was uncomfortable wearing a mask all day, sometimes even developing a rash on her face. Things have changed a lot for her. “I like it more because I learn better,” said Ann.

[5] Steven Morris, a math teacher at IDLS, has started his own YouTube channel to offer online lessons to his students. In an interview with Fox TV, Morris said he created the channel shortly after the start of the COVID-19 pandemic. “I remember how lost I was when I first started online learning. Thanks to our teacher’s channel, things are different now. My friends and I enjoy online learning a lot. It’s more autonomous, flexible and fun,” said Tom, one of Steven’s students.

Task 2: Write an article about fast food. (6 pts)

Why Do People Prefer Fast Food?

Fast food is becoming more and more popular all over the world. There are several reasons for this.

First, it is quick: people with busy lives do not have time to cook. Second, it is cheap and available everywhere. Moreover, many young people like its taste and enjoy eating it with their friends.

In my opinion, fast food is not a good habit because it contains a lot of fat and sugar and can cause obesity. We should eat it only from time to time and prefer healthy home-made meals.

Exam Tips and Key Vocabulary

What this exam tests

The reading text of the July 2021 Arts exam is about people’s experiences with online learning during the COVID-19 pandemic. The paper tests Reading, Language and Writing, so read the text twice before answering and base every answer on the text.

Key vocabulary from the text

  • online learning: التعلم عن بعد
  • pandemic: جائحة
  • logistical: لوجستي
  • barrier: عائق
  • distraction: تشتيت
  • engaged: منخرط
  • enrolled: سجل
  • supplies: لوازم
  • autonomous: مستقل
  • secure: آمن

Grammar points in this exam

  • In spite of, will and relative pronouns
  • Phrasal verbs (take after)
  • Word formation (leadership, unemployed)
  • Past continuous, present perfect and future perfect
  • Passive progressive, reported speech and so that
  • Language functions

Revise these structures: the Language section of this exam is built on them.

Writing tips

The writing paper has two tasks. In task 1, continue the paragraph about what you share on WhatsApp. In task 2, write an article explaining why people prefer fast food and give your opinion.
