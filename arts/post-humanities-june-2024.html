[1] Located in the north-west of Morocco, Chefchaouen, also known as Chaouen, attracts more and more local and foreign visitors throughout the year. The city was founded in 1471, and it has more than 42,700 inhabitants. Because of its bright blue colors, Chefchaouen is called the 'Blue Pearl'.

[2] The blue pearl of Morocco is one of the biggest tourist destinations in the world. In 2018, the city won the trophy for the best African city. A year later, it ranked 9th for having the most beautiful streets in the world. The French brand Louis Vuitton chose Chefchaouen to advertise their new Spring-Summer 2020 collection. These events celebrate the great success of the city as a renowned touristic and cultural capital.

[3] Chefchaouen has always been one of the country's most photographed cities. Thanks to its attractive scenery, tourists who visit this city are hoping for the best selfie. With more than 620,000 posts on Instagram with the hashtag #Chefchaouen, the city is now the most instagrammable city in Morocco. Influencers crowd in the city on a daily basis just to get a nice shot for their social media presence. They are more interested in the selfie than the city itself.

[4] Because of this big and harmful tourism in the city, Fodor, a travel magazine, adds the city to the list of places 'harmed by Instagram'. However, locals tell a different story. For them, the city's being famous on Instagram has created more business opportunities. Riads are renovated, more hotels have opened, and restaurants are receiving more clients. Hamza, a creative inhabitant of the Medina, for example, has turned his house into a museum. "All these people come and put photos of my house on Instagram, YouTube and Facebook. It is famous now. I have been able to have a business of my own because of it," says Hamza. To better serve tourists, some locals have learned English and others have even tried Chinese because the city is becoming so popular in Asia.

[5] If you ever plan to visit Chaouen, do your best to preserve the city's beauty. When you see shoes near the door, kindly take off yours before entering the room. When you take pictures, you need to respect the inhabitants' privacy, otherwise expect a non-welcoming smile. Please ask for permission if you want to take pictures inside the house. Task 2: Write a reply to Julia’s email. (6 pts)

Hi Julia,

Thank you for your email. I really liked your ideas. Here is how I want my country to be in 20 years.

In education, I hope all children will have free, high-quality schools, and students will learn with modern technology. In health, there will be modern hospitals in every city, so people will not need to travel far. As for the economy, I want my country to produce clean energy and create more jobs for young people. Finally, I would like cities to be greener, with more parks and public transport.

I am sure that together we can make these changes happen.
Best wishes,
Sara

Exam Tips and Key Vocabulary

What this exam tests

The reading text of the June 2024 Humanities exam is about Chefchaouen, the Blue Pearl of Morocco, and the effects of tourism and Instagram on the city. The paper tests Reading, Language and Writing, so read the text twice before answering and base every answer on the text.

Key vocabulary from the text

  • inhabitants: سكان
  • renowned: مشهور
  • scenery: مناظر طبيعية
  • instagrammable: يصلح للتصوير
  • harmful: ضار
  • renovate: يرمم
  • privacy: خصوصية
  • preserve: يحافظ على
  • opportunity: فرصة
  • destination: وجهة

Grammar points in this exam

  • Phrasal verb (look after), modal should and relative pronoun who
  • Vocabulary (disasters, renewable)
  • Word formation (healthy, performance)
  • Verb forms (gerund and future perfect)
  • Reported advice, first conditional, passive with modal and language functions

Revise these structures: the Language section of this exam is built on them.

Writing tips

The writing paper has two tasks. In task 1, complete the paragraph about things that make you happy. In task 2, reply to Julia’s email and tell her how you want your country to be 20 years from now.
