Writing: Write an email to a friend about a problem. (10 pts)

Dear Sara,

How are you? I am writing to tell you about a problem my cousin faced last year. He was in the last year of high school, and he was often late because he used to stay up late playing video games. His teacher complained to the headmaster, and his parents were told.

His father was very angry and punished him, and he felt guilty and stressed. Fortunately, his older brother talked to him calmly and helped him make a timetable for study and rest. He limited his gaming to weekends, and his marks improved a lot by the end of the term.

I think the problem was solved because someone listened to him instead of only blaming him. Write to me soon and tell me your news.

Best wishes,
Amine

Exam Tips and Key Vocabulary

What this exam tests

The reading text of the June 2009 Arts exam is about working women in Kuwait and the choice between a career and staying at home, with the voices of Batool, Reem and Aisha. The paper tests Reading, Language and Writing, so read the text twice before answering and base every answer on the text.

Key vocabulary from the text

  • career: مهنة
  • housewife: ربة بيت
  • income: دخل
  • household: الأسرة
  • guilty: مذنب
  • permit: يسمح
  • balance: توازن
  • feasible: ممكن
  • pressure: ضغط
  • generation: جيل

Grammar points in this exam

  • Collocations (have access, take place, note taking)
  • Word formation (addiction, harmful, behaviour)
  • Past continuous, past simple and future perfect
  • Relative pronouns (who, where)
  • Reported speech, passive voice and wishes
  • Linking words (as a result, though, due to)

Revise these structures: the Language section of this exam is built on them.

Writing tips

The writing task asks for an email to a friend. Start with a greeting, say why you are writing, then answer every guiding question (what, when, who, how it was solved, how you felt) in short paragraphs. Close with a friendly ending and your name.
