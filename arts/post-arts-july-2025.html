Girls of similar ages spend an hour more on social media compared to boys.

[2] Parents have many concerns about their teenagers' use of social media. For 46% of parents, these include having access to inappropriate content, wasting time and being distracted from completing homework. These parents say they are keeping a watchful eye on what their teens do on social media. Some are also imposing screen time limits on the use of the platforms. Some parents even believe that for a better protection of their kids, the law should give them full access to their teens' social media accounts.

[3] In comparing how much time teens spend on social media to the time they spend on their school work, it's not surprising that scrolling wins studying. The majority of teenagers spend about 4 hours per day on social media, but just 29% said they spend that much time doing homework. Parents express their concern about their children being distracted from completing their homework. "I think the amount of technology that kids are exposed to on a daily basis is shocking," said James Steyer, founder of Common Sense Media.

[4] Psychologists have pointed out how social media companies force teens to spend more time on the apps through their engineering and designs. Scientists conclude that teens spending too much time on social media is the result of having problems of self-control. Social media is designed to be addictive. The platforms try to get users to spend as much time as possible scrolling up and down. "They use clever marketing tactics such as frequent notifications and reports of your "likes' and 'retweets'," says John Sharry, a psychologist.

[5] To help their children with social media addiction, Dr. Prinstein, a social therapist, advises parents to start talking with their kids about their use of the platforms. "Limit screen time that interferes with at least 8 hours of sleep a night to ensure healthy brain development among teens," says doctor Prinstein.

Writing

Task 1: Complete the following paragraph. (4 pts)

First, schools can organize awareness campaigns and invite experts to explain why animals and birds are important. Second, we can use social media to share videos and pictures about endangered species. Moreover, we can plant trees and keep natural areas clean, because they are the home of many animals. Finally, the government should punish people who hunt illegally.

Task 2: Write an article about social media influencers. (6 pts)

The Positive Influence of YouTubers

Social media influencers and YouTubers have millions of followers, and they can have a positive influence on their audience.

First, many of them share educational content, such as science experiments, language lessons and tutorials, so their followers learn new things for free. Second, some influencers promote a healthy lifestyle: they show sports exercises and healthy recipes. Moreover, others raise awareness about important issues like protecting the environment or helping poor people, and they organize charity campaigns. For example, some Moroccan YouTubers collect donations for villages in need.

In conclusion, if influencers use their popularity wisely, they can inspire and help their audience.

Exam Tips and Key Vocabulary

What this exam tests

The reading text of the July 2025 Arts exam is about American teenagers’ use of social media and parents’ concerns. The paper tests Reading, Language and Writing, so read the text twice before answering and base every answer on the text.

Key vocabulary from the text

  • adolescent: مراهق
  • platform: منصة
  • inappropriate: غير لائق
  • distracted: مشتت
  • screen time: وقت الشاشة
  • scrolling: التصفح
  • addictive: يسبب الإدمان
  • tactics: أساليب
  • notification: إشعار
  • self-control: ضبط النفس

Grammar points in this exam

  • Relative adverb where and avoid + -ing
  • Vocabulary (involvement, floods)
  • Word formation (economic, preservation)
  • Past simple and present perfect
  • Conditional type 3, reported suggestions and passive future
  • Language functions (accepting an apology, agreeing)

Revise these structures: the Language section of this exam is built on them.

Writing tips

The writing paper has two tasks. In task 1, continue the paragraph with ways to raise awareness about protecting animals and birds. In task 2, write an article explaining how influencers can influence their audience in a positive way, with examples.
