Writing: Write an article about the 2014 heavy rain. (10 pts)

Heavy Rain in Morocco: Disaster and Solidarity

In 2014, heavy rain hit many areas in Morocco and caused a lot of damage. Several people died, many houses collapsed, roads and bridges were destroyed, and hundreds of families lost everything.

Fortunately, different organizations and associations volunteered to help the victims. They gave them food and blankets, and they set up tents for families who had lost their homes. Doctors also offered free medical help to the injured and to children. Young volunteers collected clothes and money and took them to the affected villages.

This disaster was sad, but it showed the solidarity of Moroccans. In conclusion, helping each other in difficult times is a duty for all of us.

Exam Tips and Key Vocabulary

What this exam tests

The reading text of the June 2015 Humanities exam is about child marriage in Morocco and how to end it. The paper tests Reading, Language and Writing, so read the text twice before answering and base every answer on the text.

Key vocabulary from the text

  • child marriage: زواج القاصرات
  • minor: قاصر
  • permission: إذن
  • dignity: كرامة
  • humiliated: مهان
  • divorce: طلاق
  • illiteracy: أمية
  • domestic violence: عنف أسري
  • guarantee: يضمن
  • demand: يطالب

Grammar points in this exam

  • Vocabulary (rural, health) and word formation (violation, wealthy)
  • Verb patterns (decide to start, aim at raising)
  • Third conditional, passive voice and reported advice
  • Relative adverb where and relative pronoun whose
  • Language functions (complaining, expressing certainty, asking for clarification)

Revise these structures: the Language section of this exam is built on them.

Writing tips

The writing task asks for an article about the 2014 heavy rain. Describe the consequences first, then explain how organizations and associations helped the victims.
