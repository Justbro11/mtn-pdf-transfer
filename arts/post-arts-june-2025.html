[1] Children in hospitals often miss fun holiday events, like Halloween. Usually, these children cannot go trick-or-treating, which is the activity children do when they go from door to door asking for candies. This is why the Walt Disney Company and Starlight Children's Foundation bring Halloween activities to them in hospitals.

[2] This year, the two organizations had fun activities, sent costumes, books and toys to 450 children's hospitals. Disney fans also contributed to this initiative by buying costumes from Disney online shops. This helped Disney to donate 20000 costumes to sick children. This is part of Disney's ongoing efforts to deliver joy to children facing serious illnesses.

[3] At Cohen Children's Hospital in New York, Disney and Starlight hosted a party. Actor Aulii Cravalho, the voice of Moana in the animated film Moana, visited the children and played with them. "Cravalho spent time singing and coloring with children in the hospital's hall before going upstairs to meet kids who couldn't leave their rooms," said a mother.

[4] Disney and Starlight initiatives are making a big impact on children's lives. They help patients feel like their favorite Disney Princess, which reduces their stress and brings joyful memories for families during their hospital stay. "After the stressful week we had, this was the biggest blessing," said a patient's mom. "The moment my daughter put on the dress, she said 'Mom, I'm a princess! It's amazing to see her happy, relaxed and motivated. It makes the hospital experience less scary, and the kids feel comfortable," added the mother.

[5] Disney's kindness isn't limited to the United States. In June 2016, Disney provided funds for Disney Fun Houses at 50 children's hospitals across China. Featuring Disney characters from beloved films, Disney Fun Houses provide young patients with a space for play activities animated by local volunteers. In South Korea, Disney created a play space at Guro Hospital. This area offers a playground for children. It is also equipped with Disney books and a TV screen displaying Disney animations. This is an example of the company's program, which is worth $100 million, to bring joy to child patients in hospitals across the globe.

[6] Disney and Starlight have worked together for more than 20 years to bring happiness to children in hospitals. Their main objective is to make the hospital experience brighter and bring smiles to kids. Collaborations like this make an enormous difference for patients facing serious illnesses. These initiatives are needed worldwide to break the routine; it's a time for kids to just be kids.

Task 2: Write an article about parents limiting social media and gaming. (6 pts)

Why Do Parents Limit Social Media and Gaming Time?

Many parents limit the time their children spend on social media and video games. There are several reasons for this.

First, parents worry about their children’s studies, because too much gaming makes them lose focus and get low marks. Second, long hours in front of a screen are bad for health: they cause eye problems, lack of sleep and lack of exercise. Moreover, some websites contain violent or inappropriate content, and parents want to protect their children from online dangers.

In my opinion, parents are right to set rules, as long as they explain their reasons and allow reasonable time for fun.

Exam Tips and Key Vocabulary

What this exam tests

The reading text of the June 2025 Arts exam is about Disney and Starlight, who bring joy to children in hospitals. The paper tests Reading, Language and Writing, so read the text twice before answering and base every answer on the text.

Key vocabulary from the text

  • trick-or-treating: طلب الحلوى في الهالوين
  • initiative: مبادرة
  • contribute: يساهم
  • donate: يتبرع
  • ongoing: متواصل
  • beloved: محبوب
  • volunteer: متطوع
  • equipped: مجهز
  • collaboration: تعاون
  • scary: مخيف

Grammar points in this exam

  • Promise + to-infinitive and relative pronoun who
  • Past perfect and wish + past
  • Word formation (responsibility, scientific)
  • Vocabulary (digital, heritage)
  • Conditional type 3, reported advice and although
  • Language functions

Revise these structures: the Language section of this exam is built on them.

Writing tips

The writing paper has two tasks. In task 1, continue the paragraph about what you like about your family. In task 2, write an article explaining why parents limit social media and gaming time and give your opinion.
