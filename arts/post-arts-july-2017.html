[1] The winner of the 2016 Global Teacher Award is a Palestinian teacher called Hanan Al Hroub. She grew up in a Palestinian refugee camp near Bethlehem and she teaches refugee pupils herself. She specializes in supporting children who have suffered from violence. She was awarded the Global Teacher Prize for her excellent contribution to the teaching profession. The finalists for this prize included teachers from India, Kenya, Finland, and the United States. The award was announced at an awards ceremony in Dubai, with a video message of congratulations sent by Prince William. He spoke of the great responsibility of teachers and how they could influence and shape a young person's life for the better.

[2] Pope Francis announced via a video conference that Hanan had won the $1 million teaching award. I would like to congratulate the teacher Hanan Al Hroub for winning this prestigious prize because of the importance that she gave to the role of play in a child's education. A child has the right to play. Part of education is to teach children how to play because they learn how to be social through games as well as learn the joy of life, Pope Francis said.

[3] Hanan became a teacher after her children and husband were shot at on their way home from school. This incident had a negative impact on her children s behavior and personality. She was left alone to support them. With no help from the educational authorities, she started inventing games at home and inviting the neighbors' children to participate. Gradually, the children's behavior and grades began to improve. Now she teaches using play at school to help pupils reject violence and tension. In her speech at the ceremony, she told the audience that teachers could change the world.

[4] I’m proud to be a Palestinian female teacher standing on this stage. I accept this as a win for all teachers in general and Palestinian teachers in particular. I tell all teachers that our job is humane, and its goals are noble. We must teach our children that our only weapon is knowledge and education," she said after receiving the award. She says she will spend the prize money on supporting her students by creating scholarships to encourage excellent ones to be teachers.

[5] The award was created by the Varkey Foundation, a non-profit organization founded by Dubai- based education entrepreneur Sunny Varkey. It aims at improving the standards of education for underprivileged children throughout the world. “ I hope that this teacher's story would inspire those looking to enter the teaching profession. My hope is that children from around the world will watch-this ceremony and think about what their own teachers do for them, said Mr. Varkey.

Task 2: Write an email to your American friend about cultural aspects. (6 pts)

Dear Jane,

Thank you for your last email. You asked me about my culture, so I would like to tell you about some aspects of life in my area.

First, our food is delicious. We often eat couscous on Fridays and drink mint tea with our guests. Second, people wear traditional clothes such as the djellaba and the caftan on special occasions. As for music, we listen to Andalusian and Amazigh music, especially at weddings and festivals.

I hope you can visit us one day and enjoy our culture.

Best wishes,
Ali

Exam Tips and Key Vocabulary

What this exam tests

The reading text of the July 2017 Arts exam is about Hanan Al Hroub, the Palestinian teacher who won the 2016 Global Teacher Prize. The paper tests Reading, Language and Writing, so read the text twice before answering and base every answer on the text.

Key vocabulary from the text

  • refugee camp: مخيم للاجئين
  • specialize: يتخصص
  • prestigious: مرموق
  • prize: جائزة
  • scholarship: منحة
  • humane: إنساني
  • noble: نبيل
  • underprivileged: محروم
  • inspire: يلهم
  • violence: عنف

Grammar points in this exam

  • Phrasal verbs (look after, find out)
  • Correct sentences: conditional type 3, future perfect, must have
  • Past simple and past perfect
  • Collocations (brain drain, adult literacy)
  • Relative adverb where, might
  • Language functions (suggesting, congratulating, requesting)

Revise these structures: the Language section of this exam is built on them.

Writing tips

The writing paper has two tasks. In task 1, continue the paragraph with causes of street children. In task 2, write an email to an American friend about food, clothes and music in your area.
