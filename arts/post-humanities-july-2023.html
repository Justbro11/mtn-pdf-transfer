[1] Ghanim Al-Muftah, who appeared at the last World Cup opening ceremony, was born in 2002 with a rare disease known as Caudal Regression Syndrome. This disease affects the lower parts of the body. However, rather than allowing this handicap to impact his life, he decided to overcome obstacles with positivity. Before being born, many doctors urged his mother to have an abortion. But she decided to have the child. After his birth, doctors thought he wouldn't live more than 15 years, but he outlived it. As he grew up, all the people expected him to use a wheelchair, but he challenged them and insisted on moving around on his hands.

[2] Ghanim lives a normal life. So, at the age of seven, he played football with his school friends, with shoes on his hands instead of his feet. He also learned to swim and do judo. Later on, he took up scuba diving, ice-hockey and mountain climbing. His dream is to climb Mount Everest. He believes that everyone has the right to dream and have ambitions.

[3] At first, it was difficult for him to integrate into the school community because of bullying from his classmates. Moreover, many schools refused to admit him. But his encouraging mom managed to change his classmates' views about him. "Without my mom's support, my education will be at stake," Ghanim said. Now he is doing his bachelor's degree in political sciences. His ambition is to work as a diplomat and later be the prime minister of Qatar.

[4] As an influencer, Ghanim is actually followed by millions all over his social media platforms. The young man has become so popular, with more than 3.4 M followers on Instagram and 1.4 M subscribers on YouTube. He was also named Ambassador of Peace in 2014. On March 18th in 2015, he won a youth award at the Arab Social Media Influencer Summit for the popularity of his Instagram account. He also won the Social Entrepreneur of the Year award in 2022.

[5] At the football World Cup opening ceremony, Ghanim shared the stage with the renowned Hollywood actor Morgan Freeman. Spectators wowed at his beautiful message. "We sent out the call; everyone is welcome. This is an invitation to the whole world. With tolerance and respect, we can live together in one big home," he said. To this invitation, Freeman replied: "What unites us here, in this moment, is so much greater than what divides us. How can we make it last longer than just today?"

Writing

Task 1: Complete the paragraph. (4 pts)

Nowadays, some people prefer to work from home for many reasons. First, they save time and money because they do not need to travel to the office. Second, they can organize their schedule and spend more time with their families. In addition, working from home is more comfortable and less stressful. Finally, it is good for the environment because there are fewer cars on the roads.

Task 2: Write an article about the rules in your school. (6 pts)

School Rules

Every school has rules, and ours is no exception. For example, students mustn’t use their mobiles in the classroom, they must wear the school uniform and they have to arrive on time.

I like the rule about mobile phones because it helps us concentrate on the lessons. I also like the rule that forbids violence, because it keeps everyone safe. However, I do not like the rule that obliges us to wear a uniform every day, because I think students should be free to choose their clothes.

In conclusion, rules are necessary, but they should be fair and explained to students.

Exam Tips and Key Vocabulary

What this exam tests

The reading text of the July 2023 Humanities exam is about Ghanim Al-Muftah, a young man born with a rare disease who became an inspiring influencer. The paper tests Reading, Language and Writing, so read the text twice before answering and base every answer on the text.

Key vocabulary from the text

  • disease: مرض
  • handicap: إعاقة
  • overcome: يتغلب على
  • urge: يحث
  • outlive: يعيش أطول من
  • bullying: تنمر
  • admit: يقبل
  • influencer: مؤثر
  • tolerance: تسامح
  • ambition: طموح

Grammar points in this exam

  • Vocabulary (equity, global, backgrounds, advances)
  • Word formation (elimination, rapidly)
  • Verb forms (gerund, infinitive of purpose, past perfect, wish)
  • Cause (because of), third conditional and passive voice
  • Relative pronoun who, phrasal verb look into, connector nevertheless and language functions

Revise these structures: the Language section of this exam is built on them.

Writing tips

The writing paper has two tasks. In task 1, complete the paragraph about why some people prefer to work from home. In task 2, write an article about the rules in your school, which you like and which you do not.
