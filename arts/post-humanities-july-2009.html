[1] Nadia was born in France and now lives in Douar Inchaden, her family's home village, in a valley in the Atlas Mountains. After studying Business Administration in Nantes (north-east of France), Nadia spent six years working in the sales department of a semiconductor factory and for an Internet provider in France.

[2] In 2006, she returned to Morocco to set up her own business in Inchaden, 40 km south of Agadir. Today, she buys prickly pears from a dozen farmers' families in the area. After collecting these pears, ten female workers gather the flowers and manually extract oil from the seeds. While previously only the fruit was sold on local markets, today Nadia also successfully exports the flowers and the oil of the prickly pears of Europe. Since last year, the company has become financially independent. Its annual profit is increasing constantly. This year, Nadia already hopes to offer permanent employment to her workers.

[3] Some of the women in her village have managed to gain a seat in the village council, previously a purely male domain. Several of the village women are divorced; others, even though they are married, have to support their whole family. They work 13 hours a day in the field, with only one day off every fortnight. In the village council, they enforced school lessons for children from the age of six and a regular waste collection service.

[4] Nadia is also a member of the village council and she advises a small cooperative of women who produce goats' cheese. She helps them advertise and sell their products. She also advises them on production hygiene in cooperation with professors from the university in Agadir. As a lecturer at the Faculty of Agriculture, she intends to popularize a large-scale production of Argan oil.

[5] The cultivation and processing of prickly pears is traditionally a woman's domain. This native plant has adapted to the natural water shortage in the region. The oil and extracts of the prickly pears and the Argan trees are sold in Europe and international companies. The extracts are used in the cosmetic and pharmaceutical industry and exported abroad. This helps women enter new sales markets and earn more money.

Writing

Writing: Write an article about civic associations. (10 pts)

The Role of Civic Associations

Civic associations play an important role in our society. They offer literacy classes to illiterate people, help the poor and the handicapped, protect the environment and defend human rights. In other words, they serve the community where the state cannot reach.

Young people can also benefit a lot from taking part in the activities of these associations. First, they learn to be good citizens who care about others. Second, they develop their personality, because they gain self-confidence and autonomy when they organize activities. Moreover, they gain new values and valuable experience that will help them in their future jobs.

In conclusion, civic associations are useful for society and for young people, so we should all encourage them and join them.

Exam Tips and Key Vocabulary

What this exam tests

The reading text of the July 2009 Humanities exam is about Nadia, a woman who set up a prickly pear business in her village. The paper tests Reading, Language and Writing, so read the text twice before answering and base every answer on the text.

Key vocabulary from the text

  • prickly pear: التين الشوكي
  • set up: أسس
  • extract: يستخرج
  • export: يصدّر
  • independent: مستقل
  • permanent employment: شغل قار
  • council: مجلس
  • cooperative: تعاونية
  • lecturer: محاضر
  • cosmetic: تجميلي

Grammar points in this exam

  • Word formation (organization, establishment, peaceful)
  • Phrasal verbs (look after, fill in, break down)
  • Passive voice, suggestions and wish
  • Past continuous and future perfect
  • Linking words (in order to, because, however)

Revise these structures: the Language section of this exam is built on them.

Writing tips

The writing task asks for an article about civic associations. Explain their role first, then the benefits for young people, using the guiding points and linking words.
