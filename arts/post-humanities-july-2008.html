[1] Souad was afraid not to finish her education. The schools were located dozens of kilometers away from her home, and she had no way to get there each day. In addition, her family couldn't afford to pay her schooling.

[2] When Souad learned about a boarding school where students live and study, she jumped at the chance to continue learning. The boarding school in Tighassaline, a small village in the Middle Atlas, was supported by an aid organization and run by a local group, the Rural Girls Scholarship Committee. The committee runs a dormitory for gifted middle school girls who cannot afford to continue studying. It provides scholarships for boarding, lodging, and financial support to cover books and fees. When Souad applied, she was accepted. She was able not only to complete middle school, but also to continue to high school.

[3] When Souad finished middle school, the school director invited her to continue living in the dormitory so that she could complete her high school education. In return, he asked her to tutor the younger girls and help them with their homework. Souad was grateful; she knew that her family was too poor to pay for her studies.

[4] "I feel that I have been given a wonderful opportunity, and I know that opportunities in life don't come twice. I have succeeded in middle school and I don't want to lead the same life as my mother. I know that education has changed my life. My interests are different from those of my sister and I have learned to make my own decisions and judgments."

[5] With tears in her eyes, Souad thanked everybody for having her opportunity to complete her secondary education. I want you to be proud of me," she said. "And I want to do something for girls and women later on."

[6] In July 2005, the first group of scholarship students graduated from Tighassaline after completing three years of middle school. The aid organization is hoping to increase the number of girls benefiting from scholarships and to aid more girls like Souad to finish their schooling.

Writing: Write a review of a film. (10 pts)

Last summer I watched the film “The Pursuit of Happyness” on TV with my family. It is a drama set in San Francisco in the 1980s, directed by Gabriele Muccino, with Will Smith as the main actor.

The story is about a poor father who struggles to raise his young son while he trains for a job as a stockbroker. They lose their home and sleep in difficult places, but he never gives up. In the end, he gets the job and becomes successful.

I liked the film very much because it is moving and teaches us to be patient and to believe in our dreams. I recommend it to everyone.

Exam Tips and Key Vocabulary

What this exam tests

The reading text of the July 2008 Humanities exam is about Souad, a girl who succeeded in her studies thanks to a scholarship. The paper tests Reading, Language and Writing, so read the text twice before answering and base every answer on the text.

Key vocabulary from the text

  • boarding school: مدرسة داخلية
  • dormitory: داخلية / سكن
  • scholarship: منحة
  • afford: يتحمل تكلفة
  • tutor: يدرّس / يعطي دروسا
  • grateful: ممتن
  • opportunity: فرصة
  • located: يقع
  • aid: مساعدة
  • graduate: يتخرج

Grammar points in this exam

  • Modals of deduction (must have done)
  • Verb patterns (managed to pass)
  • Collocations
  • Wish, reported questions and passive
  • Word formation and linking words

Revise these structures: the Language section of this exam is built on them.

Writing tips

The writing task asks you to review a film you liked. Give the title, when and where you saw it, where it is set, the type, the actors, the story and ending, and your reaction.
