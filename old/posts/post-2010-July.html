[1] Barbara is from a small town in California. Some months ago, she had a terrible argument with her parents. She read an advert in a magazine about becoming a movie actress in Hollywood and thought her dream would finally come true. She did not expect her parents to refuse. “If you were eighteen,” they told her, “you could answer the advert, and you could go for an interview – or even a casting – and try your luck in Hollywood. But you’re too young! You have to finish your studies first. You can’t give up your studies now!”

[2] Determined to realize her dream, Barbara decided to run away from home. She hitch-hiked to Los Angeles where she expected to live with some friends who had left about a year before. But she was surprised to discover that her friends had no jobs and nowhere really decent to live in. She was also disappointed to know that she had no talent to become an actress according to the Hollywood casting committee. At first, she did part-time jobs; she was an extra in a war film last month, but there was no permanent job for people like her without qualifications.

[3] Barbara has been living in Los Angeles for six months now. She has been begging in the street and sleeping wherever she can – sometimes on park benches or in cheap hotels. She used to say Los Angeles is a wonderful place. Now she wonders how she could have thought such a thing. In fact, she hates it. “There’s nothing interesting about it; the people are cold and indifferent, and the place is expensive and uninviting,” she thought to herself.

[4] When Barbara reached a desperate point, she went to a local center for the homeless. She now thinks about returning home; she misses her parents very much. But she thinks that even if she asked them to return home, they would not take her back. Two months later, she saw her parents on a TV program about missing teenagers urging her to come back home. She knew then that she was still the dear little Barbara.

Writing

Writing: Some students leave school earlier for different reasons. Write an article for your school magazine stating some of these reasons and the effects of dropping out of school. (Approximately 200 words) (10 pts)

Why Do Students Drop Out of School?

Every year, many students leave school before finishing their studies. Why does this happen, and what are the consequences?

There are several reasons. First, in some areas the school is far from home, and transport is difficult or expensive, so students give up. Second, some students have no motivation to learn because they find lessons boring or they fail their exams. Another reason is family problems, such as poverty, divorce or illness, which force young people to find a job.

Dropping out of school has serious effects. First of all, students end up with poor knowledge: they are weak in languages and literacy skills. Consequently, they do not receive a proper education. In the end, it is very difficult for them to find a good job in the future, and they often work for low salaries.

To conclude, dropping out is a real problem. Parents, teachers and the government should work together to help students stay in school and build a better future.

Exam Tips and Key Vocabulary

What this exam tests

The reading text of the July 2010 Sciences exam is about barbara, a teenager who ran away to Hollywood. The paper tests Reading, Language and Writing, so read the text twice before answering and base every answer on the text.

Key vocabulary from the text

  • argument: جدال
  • run away: يهرب
  • hitch-hike: يسافر بالتوقف
  • casting: اختبار تمثيل
  • beg: يتسول
  • homeless: بدون مأوى
  • indifferent: لامبالٍ
  • desperate: يائس
  • permanent: دائم
  • talent: موهبة

Grammar points in this exam

  • Reported speech
  • Wish clauses
  • Passive voice
  • Phrasal verbs

Revise these structures: the Language section of this exam is built on them.

Writing tips

In the writing task you write an article. Give your article a title and open with an introduction that introduces the topic. Develop two or three ideas, one per paragraph, with examples. Use linking words (First of all, Moreover, However, In conclusion) and finish with a short conclusion.
