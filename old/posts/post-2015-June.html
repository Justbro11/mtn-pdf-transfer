[4] Both disabled people and their families are forced to face difficulties on a daily basis. The government is working to change the situation. The Minister for Social Development has often publicly stressed the need to reinforce and expand the rights of disabled people. “We don’t need a charity approach; we need a law which respects international conventions and universal principles of human rights,” the minister said.

[5] One official at the ministry agrees that current laws “are incomplete and do not deal with the problem of disabled people as a whole”. He admits that Morocco still has no policies in place to allow these citizens to enjoy full rights to housing, education, work, and accessibility.

Writing: Your friend doesn’t use Facebook. Write an email telling him/her about the advantages of using this social network. (10 pts)

Subject: Why you should join Facebook

Dear Sara,

I hope you are well. I’m writing to tell you why I think you should open a Facebook account.

First of all, Facebook helps you keep in touch with friends and family, even if they live in another country. Moreover, you can share photos, videos and news with them instantly and for free. Another advantage is that you can join groups about your hobbies or studies, so you can exchange ideas and learn new things. Finally, you can follow the latest news and events, and find useful information about schools and jobs.

So why not create an account today? I’m sure you will enjoy it.

Best wishes,
Amina

Baccalaureate English National Exam Sciences June 2015: Exam Tips and Key Vocabulary

This Sciences June 2015 exam is built around a reading text about disabled people in Morocco and their daily difficulties, followed by language and writing tasks. Use the notes below after you finish the interactive practice, to review what you missed.

What this exam tests

  • Reading (15 pts): comprehension questions, true or false with justification, finding synonyms and identifying references.
  • Language (15 pts): word forms, vocabulary in context, rewriting sentences, language functions and short exchanges.
  • Writing (10 pts): an email on a topic related to the unit.

Key vocabulary from the reading text

  • disability: إعاقة
  • wheelchair: كرسي متحرك
  • accident: حادث
  • integrate: يدمج
  • marginalize: يهمّش
  • public transport: النقل العمومي
  • access: ولوج
  • rights: حقوق
  • charity: إحسان
  • policy: سياسة

Grammar points to review

  • Passive voice: be + past participle (The exam was corrected by the teachers).
  • Wish clauses: wish + past simple (present) or past perfect (past regret).
  • Reported questions: use the statement word order after the question word (Where did you go? becomes he asked where I had gone).
  • Concession: although + clause; despite / in spite of + noun or -ing.

Writing tips

  • Start with a subject line and a greeting (Dear ...), then say why you are writing in the first sentence.
  • Develop two or three ideas in short paragraphs, each with a reason or an example, using linking words (First, Moreover, Finally).
  • Finish with a polite closing (Best wishes) and your name.
