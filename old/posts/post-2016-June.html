[1] Arthur Zang, a 24-year-old Cameroonian computer engineer, invented the CardioPad. The idea for this invention came to his mind while he was watching a television program about electrocardiographs during a visit to a hospital in 2007.

[2] The CardioPad is a tablet computer. It diagnoses patients with heart diseases in remote areas where access to medical services is very restricted. When a medical examination is performed on a patient in a remote village, the results are transmitted from the nurse’s tablet to that of the doctor who then interprets them. The device is believed to be Africa’s first medical tablet that can send the results of cardiac tests to a cardiologist via internet connection. The heart specialist makes a diagnosis and sends it back with a prescription to the nurse treating the patient.

[3] “When I decided to design the tablet, I didn’t have the knowledge because I’m basically a computer science engineer. I learned a lot from an online free program on electronics provided by the Indian Institute of Technology. The funding of my project was difficult. I went to the banks, but they wanted all sorts of guarantees. So I posted a video about my project on Facebook to raise funds.” This led to a $20,000 grant from the Cameroonian government, which Zang used to produce 20 tablets, two of which are being tested in hospitals in Cameroon.

[4] The Cameroonian scientific community has recognized the CardioPad as extremely effective. Thanks to this device, many patients living in remote areas do not have to travel to urban centers to seek medical examinations. Zang believes that his invention could save numerous human lives.

[5] Mafo Abeni, a 55-year-old heart patient in a rural area of Cameroon, said “I’ve had cardiovascular disease for about a year now. It is too costly and difficult for me to see a heart specialist in the city; the CardioPad saves me time and money.”

[6] Mr. Zang says he has been contacted by private investors. But he is more interested in investors who share his vision, which is not of money, but of better ways to help improve people’s lives.

Task 2: Respond to this e-mail. (6 pts)

Dear worried father,

I am sorry to hear about your problem, but I am sure there are solutions.

First of all, you should talk calmly with your sons and explain how too much chatting and gaming can affect their studies. Moreover, you can set a clear timetable: for example, one hour of games after they finish their homework. You could also encourage them to join a sports club or a youth association so that they meet friends in real life. Finally, spend more time with them, for instance by playing a game together or going out at the weekend.

I hope my advice will help you.

Best wishes

Exam Tips and Key Vocabulary

What this exam tests

The reading text of the June 2016 Sciences exam is about cardioPad, a tablet invented to check heart health. The paper tests Reading, Language and Writing, so read the text twice before answering and base every answer on the text.

Key vocabulary from the text

  • tablet: لوحة إلكترونية
  • heart: قلب
  • inventor: مخترع
  • rural: قروي
  • diagnose: يشخّص
  • device: جهاز
  • screen: يفحص
  • invent: يخترع
  • award: جائزة
  • remote: عن بعد

Grammar points in this exam

  • Passive voice
  • Reported speech
  • Conditionals
  • Linking words

Revise these structures: the Language section of this exam is built on them.

Writing tips

In the writing task you write an article. Give your article a title and open with an introduction that introduces the topic. Develop two or three ideas, one per paragraph, with examples. Use linking words (First of all, Moreover, However, In conclusion) and finish with a short conclusion.
