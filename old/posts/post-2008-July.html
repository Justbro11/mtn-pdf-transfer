[1] Thomas Edison lit up the world with his invention of the electric light. Without him, the world might be a dark place. However, the electric light was not his only invention. He also invented the phonograph, the motion picture camera, and over 1,200 other things. About every two weeks, he created something new.

[2] The Edisons moved to Michigan when Thomas was seven. Surprisingly, he attended school for only two months. His mother, a former teacher, taught him a few things, but Thomas was mostly self-educated. His natural curiosity led him to start experimenting at a young age with electrical things at home.

[3] When he was 12 years old, he got his first job. He became a newsboy on a train that ran between Port Huron and Detroit. He set up a laboratory in a baggage car of the train to continue his experiments in his spare time. Unfortunately, his first work experience did not end well. Thomas lost his job when he accidentally set fire to the floor of the baggage car.

[4] Thomas, then, worked for five years as a telegraph operator, but he continued to spend much of his time conducting experiments. He got his first patent in 1868 for a vote recorder run by electricity. In 1870, he sold another invention, a stock-ticker, for $40,000. A stock-ticker is a machine that automatically prints stock prices on a tape. He was then able to build his first shop in New Jersey.

[5] Edison was handicapped; he was totally deaf in one ear and hard of hearing in the other, but he thought of his deafness as a blessing in many ways. It kept conversations short so that he could have more time for work. He called himself a “two-shift man” because he worked 16 out of 24 hours. Edison died at the age of 84 on October 18th, 1931, in New Jersey. He left numerous inventions that improved the quality of life all over the world.

A. Which of the following would be the best title for this passage? (2 pts)

1. The story of a genius
Edison invented over 1,200 things and worked extremely hard.

B. Are these sentences true or false? Justify. (4 pts)

  1. False. “He also invented the phonograph, the motion picture camera, and over 1,200 other things.” (paragraph 1)
  2. False. He started experimenting “at a young age with electrical things at home”. (paragraph 2)
  3. True. “When he was 12 years old, he got his first job.” (paragraph 3)
  4. True. “He called himself a ‘two-shift man’ because he worked 16 out of 24 hours.” (paragraph 5)

Baccalaureate English National Exam Sciences July 2008: Exam Tips and Key Vocabulary

This Sciences July 2008 exam is built around a reading text about thomas Edison, the inventor (only the reading part of this paper is available). Use the notes below after you finish the interactive practice, to review what you missed.

What this exam tests

  • Reading (15 pts): comprehension questions, true or false with justification, finding synonyms and identifying references.

Key vocabulary from the reading text

  • invention: اختراع
  • self-educated: متعلم بنفسه
  • curiosity: فضول
  • experiment: تجربة
  • laboratory: مختبر
  • patent: براءة اختراع
  • telegraph: تلغراف
  • deaf: أصم
  • blessing: نعمة
  • numerous: عديد

Reading tips

  • Justify every true or false answer with a sentence from the text.
  • Use the paragraph number to find a word, and for reference questions read the sentence before the underlined word.
  • Base all your answers on the text, in your own words when asked.
