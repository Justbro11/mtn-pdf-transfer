Writing

Writing: The emigration of skilled workers is a real problem for their countries of origin. Write an email to the editor of the website www.globaldebate.org in which you explain why these people emigrate and suggest ways to encourage them to stay in their own countries. (10 pts)

Subject: The emigration of skilled workers

Dear editor,

I am writing about the emigration of skilled workers, which is a real problem for developing countries.

There are several reasons why these people leave. First, they look for higher salaries, because wages in their own countries are often low. Second, they want better working conditions and more opportunities for research and promotion. Finally, some of them are attracted by a better quality of life abroad.

To encourage them to stay, governments should increase salaries and offer them modern equipment and laboratories. They should also create more jobs and give skilled workers a chance to take part in important projects. In addition, companies could offer them bonuses and good housing.

I believe that if these measures are taken, many experts will choose to serve their own countries.

Yours faithfully,
Hamza

Exam Tips and Key Vocabulary

What this exam tests

The reading text of the June 2012 Sciences exam is about ruth, a woman who studies nursing late in life. The paper tests Reading, Language and Writing, so read the text twice before answering and base every answer on the text.

Key vocabulary from the text

  • certificate: شهادة
  • vocational school: مدرسة مهنية
  • scholarship: منحة
  • elderly: المسنون
  • salary: أجر
  • unskilled: غير ماهر
  • patience: صبر
  • drop out: ينقطع عن الدراسة
  • support: يعيل
  • improve: يحسّن

Grammar points in this exam

  • Reported speech
  • Passive voice
  • Third conditional
  • Phrasal verbs

Revise these structures: the Language section of this exam is built on them.

Writing tips

In the writing task you write an email. Start with a subject line and a greeting (Dear ...). Say why you are writing in the first sentence, then develop two or three ideas in short paragraphs, each with a reason or an example. Close with a polite ending (Best wishes) and your name.
