Task 2: Write an article to your online school forum. (6 pts)

How Travelling Benefits Young People

Travelling is more than a holiday; it can change the way young people see the world.

First of all, travelling helps young people learn about other cultures, languages and traditions, so they become more open-minded. Moreover, it teaches them to be independent because they must solve problems on their own, such as finding a hotel or managing their money. Travelling also helps them practise foreign languages in real situations. Finally, it gives them unforgettable memories and new friends from different countries.

In conclusion, travelling is a school of life, and every young person should travel whenever possible.

Baccalaureate English National Exam Sciences July 2019: Exam Tips and Key Vocabulary

This Sciences July 2019 exam is built around a reading text about sophia the robot and artificial intelligence, followed by language and writing tasks. Use the notes below after you finish the interactive practice, to review what you missed.

What this exam tests

  • Reading (15 pts): comprehension questions, true or false with justification, finding synonyms and identifying references.
  • Language (15 pts): word forms, vocabulary in context, rewriting sentences, language functions and short exchanges.
  • Writing (10 pts): an article on a topic related to the unit.

Key vocabulary from the reading text

  • robot: روبوت
  • artificial intelligence: الذكاء الاصطناعي
  • citizenship: الجنسية
  • human-like: شبيه بالإنسان
  • expression: تعبير
  • develop: يطوّر
  • replace: يعوّض
  • invent: يخترع
  • technology: تكنولوجيا
  • future: مستقبل

Grammar points to review

  • Passive voice: be + past participle (The exam was corrected by the teachers).
  • Reported speech: shift the tense back and change pronouns and time words (I will come becomes she said she would come).
  • Conditionals: check the type: If + present, will; if + past simple, would; if + past perfect, would have.
  • Linking words: use because of, in order to, as a result, although, so as to correctly.

Writing tips

  • Give your article a title and open with an introduction that presents the topic.
  • Develop two or three ideas, one per paragraph, with examples and linking words (First of all, Moreover, However).
  • Finish with a short conclusion that gives your opinion or advice.
